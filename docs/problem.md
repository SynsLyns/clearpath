# ClearPath problem framing

The current process is assumed to move campaign copy through email and spreadsheets. That creates avoidable intake gaps, queue delays, repeated rework, and status pings. The figures below are illustrative assumptions for the take-home, not observed customer or production data.

| Stage | Baseline (assumed) | With ClearPath (assumed) | Product lever |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Submission and intake | About 0.5 day (chasing missing information by email) | Minutes | Structured form with required fields |
| Triage and assignment | About 1 day | None | Risk-sorted queue, SLA aging, auto-clear |
| First-pass review | About 1 day | Hours, and zero for clean creatives | Pre-flagged issues with snippets, auto-clear |
| Rework | About 2 days (multiple email rounds) | Roughly half | Self-check before submit, resubmit with change diff |
| Status ping | About 0.5 day (people asking for updates) | None | Self-serve status list and audit trail |

The assumed baseline totals about five calendar days. With ClearPath, clean creative can bypass a human queue except for the QA sample, and a flagged submission has its issues and reviewer feedback attached to the creative. The target is a shorter, more predictable loop—not a promise that every submission will be approved or decided in a fixed time.
